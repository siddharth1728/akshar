# SENSEFORGE: Core Research Questions

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Authors:** SENSEFORGE Principal Research Architecture Group  

---

## 1. Primary Research Question (RQ1: Construct Validity of Discovered Measurements)

> **RQ1:** Can an evolutionary search process over a typed symbolic domain-specific language (DSL) discover mathematical operators that extract statistically significant predictive information from a partially observable dynamical environment, compared to classical delay embeddings and autoregressive baselines?

- **Sub-question 1.1:** What is the minimal expressivity required in the measurement grammar (e.g., temporal convolution, non-linear difference ratios, statistical windowing) to reconstruct latent state topologies from degenerate projections?
- **Sub-question 1.2:** How do we penalize operator Kolmogorov complexity (expression tree size and computational FLOPs) such that discovered instruments capture parsimonious invariants rather than memorizing local sample trajectories?

---

## 2. Experimental Probe Question (RQ2: Active vs. Passive Discovery)

> **RQ2:** Does the co-evolution of active intervention protocols (bounded perturbation sequences) alongside receptive measurement operators achieve superior latent state disambiguation compared to passive observation under identical computational budgets?

- **Sub-question 2.1:** What intervention topologies (impulse, sinusoidal sweeps, step responses, state-conditional pulses) most effectively decouple aliased simulation modes?
- **Sub-question 2.2:** Can an autonomous agent discover that an environment has non-linear hysteretic memory without being informed that hysteresis exists?

---

## 3. Falsification and Robustness Question (RQ3: Resilience to Spurious Invariants)

> **RQ3:** To what extent do candidate measurement operators withstand rigorous adversarial falsification, including phase-randomized surrogate data, noise injection, and unseen dynamical seed spaces?

- **Sub-question 3.1:** What proportion of evolutionary candidates that appear promising on training runs are rejected as spurious by permutation null-tests?
- **Sub-question 3.2:** How does the introduction of stochastic sensor noise at the observation firewall impact the convergence rate and survival of candidate instruments?

---

## 4. Transfer and Generalization Question (RQ4: Cross-Family Invariance)

> **RQ4:** Can measurement mechanisms evolved within one procedural environment family (e.g., coupled non-linear oscillators) provide non-trivial predictive utility when deployed into a structurally distinct environment family (e.g., reaction-diffusion grids or thermodynamic particulate fields)?

- **Sub-question 4.1:** Which mathematical primitive operators exhibit the highest rate of cross-family transfer?
- **Sub-question 4.2:** Can we quantify an empirical "Measurement Transferability Index" (MTI) based on mutual information gain across disjoint Hamiltonian / dissipative dynamical systems?
